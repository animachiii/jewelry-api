Background removal / replacement

Independent of the four-angle flow above — one photo in, one photo out. See docs/api-routes.md's "Background Operations" section.

Also calls the real pipeline — a real, billed Gemini call plus a QA scoring call. Output has no alpha channel: "remove" means a flat/solid backdrop, not a transparent cutout (see docs/decisions/0002-background-removal-approach.md).

Companion-piece match (MATCH)

One uploaded style-reference photo in, 1-4 generated companion-piece variants out — a different, matching item, not the same piece restaged. See docs/api-routes.md's "Companion-Piece Generation" section.

Calls the real pipeline — a real, billed Gemini call per variant. No QA gate; ships straight to COMPLETED.

Masked gemstone recolor (RECOLOR)

One uploaded source photo + one uploaded mask in, one recolored photo out — everything outside the mask is byte-identical to the source. See docs/api-routes.md's "Masked Gemstone Recolor" section.

Calls the real pipeline — a real, billed Gemini call. Mask must be a single-channel 8-bit grayscale PNG, same dimensions as the source, binary (0/255) values only, 0.5%-60% white coverage. No QA gate; ships straight to COMPLETED.

Two-piece combination (MIX)

Two photos + one mask each in, one newly designed piece out. Paint the element you want from each photo; Gemini designs a single new piece combining the two. Nothing is cut and pasted — the result is a design concept, not a photo of either piece you uploaded. See docs/api-routes.md's "Two-Piece Combination" section.

Calls the real pipeline — a real, billed Gemini call. The output is generated, so it may idealize details neither physical piece has: treat it as a mockup, not a photograph of goods in hand. Both masks follow RECOLOR's same PNG mask contract, each checked against its own photo's dimensions. No QA gate; ships straight to COMPLETED.

Cleanup then angles (GENERATE_WITH_CLEANUP)

One uploaded photo in — background-cleaned internally, then 1-4 catalogue angles generated from that cleaned image. See docs/api-routes.md's "Cleanup + Angle Generation" section.

Calls the real pipeline — a real, billed cleanup call plus one real, billed call per requested angle. The cleaned intermediate photo is never returned; only the angle outputs below are client-facing. No QA gate on either step.

Ops — re-score flagged background QA

POST /api/v2/internal/qa/rescore-flagged-background — ops scope, separate from the client-scope key above. Re-runs the QA judge over background-operation sub-jobs a broken judge flagged; see docs/api-routes.md and docs/business-rules.md §13.

Acts on live production rows. Does not touch attempt_count — this is a re-score of the judge's own past mistake, not a client-initiated retry.
